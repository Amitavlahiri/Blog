About Me

Hi, I'm Amitav.

I'm a student and screenwriter based in New York City, currently studying at the Dwight School. Driven by a love for storytelling and visual media, I've developed a voice that gravitates toward the silent and the strange: ordinary moments that turn out to be far more meaningful than they first appear.

My writing keeps circling back to grief, memory, and the surreal texture of everyday life. I'm drawn to stories, and hope to make films, where the uncanny and the mundane sit right next to each other.

Alongside screenwriting, I write op-eds on identity and originality, exploring the ideas that quietly shape how we see ourselves, as well as reviews of the films and shows that stay with me longest.

If something here resonates, or you just want to talk film, feel free to reach out.
